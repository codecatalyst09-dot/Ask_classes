Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join the live online class sessions? ▾

You can join the live online sessions directly from the "Today's Session" tab by clicking on the active "Join Class" button. Classes usually activate 10 minutes prior to their start time.

Can I access recordings of missed live classes? ▾

Yes, all live class recordings are archived and made available within 2 hours under the "My Live Classes" or "Course Materials" sections for self-study and revision.

How can I submit my assignments? ▾

Assignments can be uploaded through the "My Courses & Assignments" page under each specific course card. Select "Upload Work", choose your file (PDF or Image), and click submit.

Who should I contact if I face tech issues during a class? ▾

For immediate tech support, use the floating chat widget on the bottom right of the screen. Our technical team is online during live sessions to assist you immediately.
